About me

I am a Lecturer and a member of the pure mathematics research group at the University of Sydney.
Previously, I was a research fellow at the University of Melbourne, MPIM Bonn and IBS-CGP Pohang.
I received Ph.D. in 2018 from the University of Minnesota under the supervision of Craig Westerland.

I'm interested in the arithmetic of algebraic varieties and their moduli spaces. My work focuses on rational points of moduli stacks over function fields (equivalently, families of varieties over curves) and on the height moduli spaces that give the totality of such families a geometry of its own.

"Elliptic curves over k(t), 𝔽q(t), ℂ(t): Height moduli, exact counts, rank jumps" at Melbourne 2026
